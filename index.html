01 / THE FOUNDATION

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity should be, too.

For children and teenagers in Nigeria, football can open a door. Midas FA Foundation helps them take the next step through coaching, education, practical skills and mentorship.

We believe a young person’s progress matters on the pitch, in the classroom and in their community.

02 / OUR PROGRAMMES

01

Football development
