01 / The Foundation

Talent is
everywhere.
Opportunity
should be too.

For children and teenagers in Nigeria, football can be the beginning of something much bigger. Midas FA Foundation supports that journey through coaching, education, practical skills and mentorship.

Progress matters on the pitch, in the classroom and in the community. The goal is not simply to create better footballers, but to help young people build stronger futures.

02 / Our programmes

Built for life.
Powered by
football.

Four connected areas of development give young people the support to grow on the pitch and beyond it.

01

Football development

Structured coaching, match experience, teamwork, discipline and competitive opportunities.

02

Education support

Support that encourages young players to develop academically alongside their football journey.

03

Vocational & digital skills

Practical learning that can create more opportunities beyond competitive sport.

04

Mentorship & life skills

Personal development focused on confidence, discipline, leadership and responsible decision-making.

03

Registration

Confirm the academy requirements and complete the agreed registration process.

04

Development

Begin a journey involving football, learning, mentorship and personal development.
