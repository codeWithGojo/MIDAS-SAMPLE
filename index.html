01 / The foundation

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity should be, too.

For children and teenagers in Nigeria, football can open a door. Midas FA Foundation helps them take the next step through coaching, education, practical skills and mentorship.

We believe a young person’s progress matters on the pitch, in the classroom and in their community.

02 / Our programmes

Built for life.
Powered by football.

Four ways to help a young person find their feet and move forward.

01

Football development

Build technique, teamwork and discipline through coaching and opportunities to compete.

02

Education support

Support learning and exam participation, helping young people progress beyond their sporting ambitions.

03

Vocational & digital skills

Develop practical abilities that create possibilities alongside a career in football.

04

Mentorship & life skills

Help young people grow in confidence, make thoughtful decisions and contribute to their communities.
