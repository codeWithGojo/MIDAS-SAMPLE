At Midas FA Foundation, our numbers reflect more than growth — they represent dedication, progress, and real impact.

We simplify player enquiries and registrations, improve visibility, and better communicate our programmes and impact.

Every player trained, every exam passed, every family supported reminds us why we do this. These results are proof of our commitment to developing the next generation of footballers and leaders.
